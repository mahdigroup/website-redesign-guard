# The checks a report cannot finish for you

Use this alongside the comparison report. Record the tester, date and evidence for your own launch.

1. Check the priority journeys on a real phone and desktop: navigation, service selection and contact.
2. Submit an authorised, clearly labelled test enquiry. Confirm it is saved, reaches the correct inbox, includes the reviewed details, and handles errors without losing the visitor's text.
3. Confirm business name, address, telephone, email, opening hours and service details with the owner.
4. Review intentional URL changes and retirements, including old-host redirects for a domain move.
5. Check production indexing directives, canonicals, sitemap and HTTPS. Keep staging access controlled.
6. Inspect page layouts, keyboard access, visible focus, contrast, labels and reduced-motion behaviour. An HTML label check is not a complete accessibility audit.
7. Share an approved page privately to check its real social preview. Platform caches may retain older images.
8. If applicable, test checkout, payment and fulfilment in the provider's authorised test environment.
9. Verify the actual analytics/consent setup, authenticated mail transport, backup and rollback arrangements.
10. Review real-user performance and Search Console after launch. Laboratory checks cannot establish future rankings, traffic or enquiry quality.

Official migration guidance: [Google Search Central — site moves](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes).
